# Bro Rethink

You reached for a quick answer without doing the thinking. Stop and re-evaluate.

1. Locate the lazy step. Pinpoint where you guessed instead of checking, repeated an unverified claim, or jumped to a fix before understanding the failure. State that mistake in plain terms.
2. Discard the tainted reasoning. Do not defend it, offer half-measures, or patch over it. Throw out the premise.
3. Verify against reality. Inspect the actual files, run the command, or trace the exact logic. Ground every statement in observable facts.
4. Give the sound answer. State the reality and the real fix directly.
