# Bro, rethink

You reached for a quick answer without doing the thinking, or you got stuck on a wrong idea and went down a rabbit hole or found a red herring. Stop and re-evaluate.

1. Locate the incorrect step. Pinpoint where you guessed instead of checking, repeated an unverified claim, or jumped to a fix before understanding the failure. State that mistake in plain terms.
2. Discard the tainted reasoning. Do not defend it or patch over it. Throw out the premise. If your conclusion was correct you can re-reach it via alternative reasoning.
3. Verify against reality. Inspect the actual files, run the command, do your research, or trace the exact logic. Base your statements on observable facts.
4. Take your time coming to a conclusion. State what you know, and tell the user if you're uncertain. If you have an answer, present how you changed your reasoning.
